FAQ

What do I need to use it?

Microsoft Excel 2019 or newer / Microsoft 365, or a free Google account for Google Sheets. Word documents open in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

How do I get the files?

Right after payment you receive a download link on screen and by email.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep the files.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. You set your own currency symbol and tax/VAT rate. Distances work in km or miles.

Can I customise it?

Yes. Rename services, change prices, add your logo. Sheets are unlocked.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. The tools help you organise your numbers. Please check tax and legal requirements in your country with a professional.
